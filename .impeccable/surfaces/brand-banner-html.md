## Scope

YouTube channel identity for Second Look: the profile logo (800×800 PNG, shown as a circle down to about 48px) and the channel banner (2560×1440 PNG; the central 1546×423 safe area is all a phone shows). Mode: persuade. A first-time viewer should know in two seconds that this is a calm, careful science channel.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Second Look is the observer's almanac: the same sky checked night after night, written down. It refuses the space-channel default of a nebula photo, neon type and a glowing planet.

OWN-WORLD: The almanac's own materials. Navy book-cloth ground, page-white ink and rubric red. Red is reserved by law for "the second look": one red element per artifact, never decoration. Discs and tabular figures, flat colour, no gradients and no glow. One heavy wide grotesque for the wordmark and one workhorse sans for text, both OFL.

STORY: The viewer sees two looks overlap, and the one thing both of them catch is marked in red. They understand that this channel looks again at things you already know, and they subscribe.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo: on navy, two page-white rings overlap side by side, symmetric and centred, spanning about 68% of the width of the circle; the lens they share is red. Banner safe area: the wordmark on the left, one plain sentence under it, and on the right a single almanac row of moon phases from new to full, with lunar-day figures beneath; the full Moon at the end is red, the banner's one reveal. The schedule sits under the row.

FORM: Almanac/observer's log, position 7 on my ordered list (assigned). Raised by: arcade palette law (red reserved for the one reveal), cloud-edge confinement (colour stays in one place; the rest is navy and white).
