# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: brand assets (logo, banner) are authored as HTML/SVG and rasterised to PNG with headless Microsoft Edge, which is already on this machine; no web app is built.

## Users

Curious adults worldwide who watch in English, mostly casually on phones: scrolling Shorts, or watching an 8–10 minute explainer when a topic catches them. They want to understand something they took for granted, without hype or padding.

## Product Purpose

**Second Look** is a faceless YouTube channel publishing one Short a day and two 8–10 minute videos a week about science facts: space, the human body, nature and how things work. The yt-autopilot pipeline in this repository writes, voices, edits and uploads the videos, and a human reviews every script. Success means viewers come back because explanations are accurate, clear and quietly surprising. The YouTube Partner Program needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 long-form watch hours.

## Positioning

The channel takes things viewers have seen a thousand times (their own eyes, the Moon, a ticking clock) and explains what is really going on underneath, in a calm, warm voice with no clickbait. Every fact is checked by a human before publishing, and footage is credited.

## Operating Context

- The channel is a rename of the existing YouTube channel "Capitalist Kitchen" (@capitalistkitchen), which has 7 older off-niche videos.
- Brand assets live on YouTube: the profile picture is shown as a circle, as small as about 48 px; the banner is 2560×1440, but only the central 1546×423 safe area shows on every device.
- Video thumbnails are generated by `src/thumbnail.py`, and captions and cards by `src/assemble.py`. They currently use Anton, Bebas Neue and Montserrat with a yellow accent (#FFD60A).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Zero extra cost: free and open-source tools and OFL fonts only.
- Narration is an AI voice (Kokoro af_heart); scripts are AI-written and human-reviewed; AI use is disclosed on every video.
- Visuals are public-domain or CC-licensed stock (Wikimedia Commons, NASA), credited in descriptions.
- Pace is capped at 7 Shorts and 2 long videos a week.
- Open: the channel handle (suggested @secondlookscience; availability not yet checked).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Second Look**.
- Voice: curious, clear, warm; no hype, no clickbait lies.

## Evidence on Hand

- Published: "On Venus, a Day Lasts Longer Than a Year" (https://youtu.be/9e0mVMlPL1o).
- Produced: "Your Eyes Lie to You: How Your Brain Builds the World You See" (long video, scheduled for 3 Nov 2026).
- No subscriber, view or testimonial claims may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Accuracy before reach: a doubtful claim gets cut.
2. Make the familiar strange: start from something the viewer already knows.
3. Calm confidence: wonder carried by clarity, not volume.
4. Honest about how it's made: AI disclosure and source credits are always on.
