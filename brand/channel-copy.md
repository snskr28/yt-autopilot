## Description (YouTube allows 1,000 characters; this is about 560)
Things you've seen a thousand times, explained properly.

Second Look takes the familiar (your own eyes, the Moon, a ticking clock, a drop of honey) and shows what is really going on underneath. Space, the human body, nature and how everyday things work, in calm, clear explanations with no hype.

New Short every day. Longer deep dives every Tuesday and Friday, 18:00 IST.

How it's made: narration uses an AI voice and scripts are drafted with AI, but every script is read and fact-checked by a person before it is published. Footage and images come from public-domain and Creative Commons sources, credited in each video's description.
